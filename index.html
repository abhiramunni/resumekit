    2 Summary aim for 20–60 words

    0 words. A good summary names your field, one proof point with a number, and the role you want next.

    3 Skills at least 5, most relevant first

    List tools you can actually use on day one. Match the words in the job post — the software looks for them.

    4 Experience & projects bullets, not duties

    One bullet per line inside each card. Start every bullet with an action verb and end it with a number — "Built X, used by 400+ students" beats "Responsible for the website".

    5 Education degree · college · years

    Freshers: keep education near the top of the page. If your degree is still in progress, say so — "B.Tech IT, 2021–2025 (degree awaited)" is honest and common.
